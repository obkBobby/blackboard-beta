Misdirection

·

Sort the 16 cards into four groups of four. Don't let this game play in your face.

Regular, Honors, AP: three different boards. All are open. Each saves separately.

How to play

Choose four cards with one shared connection, then Turn It In. Find all four groups to clear the board. You have four chances, so take your time!

Example RAIN can go before BOW, COAT, DROP, and FALL to make rainbow, raincoat, raindrop, and rainfall. Some groups use meanings; others join words, change spacing, or use sounds.

Seeing "One away"? Three of your picks belong together. Swap one card and try again. Need a little help? Tap Pass Me a Note for a clue. You can ask for a stronger clue, then reveal one card if you're still stuck. Take only the help you want. Hints won't use up your chances.
